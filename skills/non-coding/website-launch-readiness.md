# Skill: Website Launch Readiness

## Purpose

Use this skill before pushing a client website or web project live.

## Good use cases

- new site launches
- redesign cutovers
- landing-page go-live
- domain or hosting migrations
- staged releases that need launch approval

## Workflow

1. Confirm launch scope and target environment.
2. Verify pages, routes, and links.
3. Verify forms, notifications, and integrations.
4. Verify metadata, indexing controls, and social previews.
5. Verify responsiveness and basic accessibility.
6. Verify analytics, tags, or tracking scripts.
7. Verify domain, SSL, redirects, and environment variables.
8. Produce a final go-live checklist and unresolved risk note.

## Output format

- environment
- scope being launched
- checklist results
- blockers
- known risks
- launch owner
- rollback note

## Guardrails

- do not treat deploy success as launch success
- verify forms and contact paths explicitly
- verify staging-vs-production environment assumptions
- list what still needs manual checking if full verification is not possible
