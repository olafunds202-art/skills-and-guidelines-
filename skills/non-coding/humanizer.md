# Skill: Humanizer

## Purpose

Use this skill whenever text needs to sound less like generic AI writing and more like natural human prose.

Apply it when the user asks to:

- humanize text
- de-AI-ify text
- make something sound more natural
- remove AI tells
- polish a draft without changing its meaning

It is also useful as a final editing pass on longer prose before delivery.

## Core rule

Change **how** the text is written, not **what** it says.

- preserve meaning
- preserve facts
- preserve the intended message
- do not add new claims, examples, sources, or evidence

## Process

1. Read the draft once for meaning only.
2. Identify AI-like patterns and remove them one by one.
3. Read again for rhythm and naturalness.
4. Check that no facts or claims were added or removed.

## Common AI tells to cut

Replace or remove inflated wording such as:

`delve, crucial, pivotal, robust, intricate, tapestry, testament, underscore, highlight as significance, showcase, garner, bolstered, fostering, enhance, align with, interplay, meticulous, landscape as abstraction, vibrant, groundbreaking, renowned, diverse array, valuable insights, deep dive`

Also cut or simplify:

- `Additionally,` at the start of sentences
- `In conclusion`, `In summary`, `Overall` as formal wrap-ups
- stiff synonyms like `utilize`, `authored`, `relocated`, `attempted`

## Sentence patterns to remove

- empty significance framing
- vague source language like `experts say` with no real source
- floating `-ing` clauses that pretend to add importance
- `not just X, but Y` constructions
- padded rule-of-three phrasing
- generic challenge/future-outlook endings
- speculation filling a knowledge gap

## Formatting rules

- avoid excessive bolding
- avoid formal conclusion headers unless actually needed
- do not overuse em dashes
- use sentence case headings
- vary sentence length naturally
- remove robotic helper phrases like `Certainly`, `Here’s a breakdown`, `I hope this helps` when polishing content

## What not to do

- do not invent facts
- do not change the underlying position or conclusion
- do not rewrite quoted material as if it were your own words
- do not flatten the text so much that it loses needed detail

## Final check

Ask:

- did any fact get removed?
- did any new fact get added?
- does it sound like something a real person would naturally say?
