# Skill: Debugging and Investigation

## Purpose

Use this skill when something is broken, inconsistent, flaky, or poorly understood.

## Workflow

1. Restate the observed problem.
2. Define expected behavior.
3. Reproduce the issue reliably.
4. Narrow the failure surface.
5. Collect logs, traces, inputs, and recent changes.
6. Form and test hypotheses one at a time.
7. Isolate the root cause or the smallest failing boundary.
8. Recommend the fix, workaround, or next experiment.

## Investigation principles

- reproduce before theorizing too much
- change one variable at a time
- distinguish symptom from cause
- keep notes of failed hypotheses to avoid loops
- prefer evidence over intuition when they conflict

## Useful outputs

- reproduction steps
- observed vs expected behavior
- narrowed component or boundary
- likely root cause
- recommended fix path
- remaining unknowns
